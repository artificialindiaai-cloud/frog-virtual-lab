Tip: set 1 V, press Single stimulus to see latent period, contraction and relaxation. Then run a train at 1 Hz (staircase, then fatigue), 15 Hz (incomplete tetanus) and 50 Hz (complete tetanus).
Pearls: Below threshold no response. Between threshold and maximal, force rises as more fibres are recruited. Beyond maximal, no further rise. Skeletal muscle has a short refractory period, so twitches summate and fuse into tetanus.
